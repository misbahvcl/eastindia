About MyEastIndia

MyEastIndia is an India Licensed Exporter dedicated to bridging the gap between India's rich agricultural heritage and the rigorous demands of the global market. We specialize in the sourcing, quality control, and global distribution of fresh fruits, vegetables, premium pulses, and authentic spices.

For international B2B importers, sourcing overseas can mean navigating quality inconsistencies and logistical blind spots. We eliminate that risk — operating strategically out of India's major shipping hubs, Cochin, Trivandrum and Mumbai, to provide seamless, end-to-end export solutions.

Beyond simply supplying raw materials, we act as your dedicated on-ground partner — whether you need a reliable direct supplier, an expert export consultant to navigate Indian trade regulations, or a rigorous third-party QA/QC agent to verify your existing shipments.

Our Services

Comprehensive, end-to-end B2B supply chain solutions for premium Indian fresh produce, pulses and spices.

We provide comprehensive, end-to-end B2B supply chain solutions for premium fresh fruits, vegetables, pulses, and spices. Operating out of strategic hubs in Cochin, Trivandrum, and Mumbai, we serve as both a direct supplier and an independent QA/QC agent for international buyers. We are committed to a strict zero-adulteration and pesticide-free policy, sourcing organically grown produce directly from trusted farms. Whether you are importing into the highly regulated markets of Europe, the fast-paced hubs of the Middle East, or growing sectors across Asia and Africa, our expert consulting team seamlessly navigates Indian trade compliance to deliver exactly what your regional market demands.

To guarantee maximum durability and zero perishability during global transit, we integrate advanced preservation technology and logistics at every step of the export process. From immediate pre-cooling of fresh harvests to specialized stuffing solutions utilizing both Dry and Reefer containers, our cold-chain and moisture-control methods are world-class. We leverage strong coordination with multiple premier shipping lines to optimize routing, but our true differentiator is absolute transparency. Our clients receive live visual updates during the container stuffing and packing process, and every single package is secured with our tamper-evident MyEastIndia QC seal before export, ensuring total peace of mind from the moment it leaves our facility until it reaches your warehouse.
